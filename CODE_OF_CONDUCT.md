# Code of conduct

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and
focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing private
information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@marona.ai`.
